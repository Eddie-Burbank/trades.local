The Post-Money Promise

We don’t want your data. We don’t want your money.

Trades.Local is an experiment in decentralized mutual aid. It is a digital corkboard designed for a post-money world, where goods, skills, and time are traded directly between neighbors.

How We Protect Your Privacy

  • Zero Accounts: There are no profiles, passwords, or tracking pixels.
  • Zero Databases: When a trade is accepted, the public listing evaporates.
  • Ephemeral Chat: Communications happen in self-destructing, RAM-only web sockets. When the trade is over, the chat is permanently wiped. We cannot read your messages, and no algorithm can mine them.

The Honor System & Your Safety

We have taken every architectural measure possible to design a safe, self-policing community. Trust is built through a localized Honor System. When you successfully complete a trade in person, a cryptographically signed "stamp" is saved directly to your device's browser. This proves to your neighbors that you are reliable, without requiring a central database to track who you are. Harmful posts can also be permanently removed via anonymous community voting.

The Corkboard Disclaimer (Please Read)

However, we provide the corkboard and nothing else. Because we do not track our users, we cannot monitor listings, mediate disputes, or verify the true identity of anyone on this platform. We assume no legal liability for any trades, meetups, or interactions that originate here.

By entering, you agree to take absolute personal responsibility for your safety. Always meet strangers in well-lit, highly populated public spaces (like a library or local cafe) during daylight hours. If an interaction feels unsafe, walk away and contact local authorities. Assume most people are good, but protect yourself accordingly.
